# Drill-down and drill-up – steps for the demo (kept off the report page on purpose)

**Open first:** the **EDA & PASSENGER ANALYSIS** tab (second tab at the bottom).
**Chart:** the top-left chart, titled *"Satisfaction by Travel Class"* (bars: Business, Economy, Economy Plus).
Its axis is the hierarchy **Class → Type of Travel**.

1. Move the mouse over the chart. A small icon bar appears just above the chart's top-right corner. From the left it shows:
   **↑ Drill up**, **↓ Drill down (mode)**, **⇊ Go to the next level**, **⑂ Expand all down one level**, then Filter / Focus mode / More.
2. Click the single **↓** icon. It turns dark – drill-down mode is ON.
3. Click the **Business** column. The chart now shows **Business** split into its **Type of Travel** values (Business / Personal).
4. **Drill up:** click **↑** (the first icon). The chart goes back to Business / Economy / Economy Plus.
5. Quick alternative: click **⇊** to split *every* class by Type of Travel at once; click **↑** to return.
6. Click the single **↓** again to turn drill mode OFF (so that clicking a bar selects it instead of drilling).

If the icons do not appear: click the chart once first, then move the mouse onto it.
