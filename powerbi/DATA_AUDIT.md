### Discrepancy to be aware of: Average Service Rating 3.28 vs 3.21
A teammate card shows 3.21. That is `avg_service_rating` (mean 3.2120) from `satisfaction_tiers.py`, which averages only SIX attributes (Wi-Fi, online boarding, seat comfort, food and drink, entertainment, cleanliness) per passenger, zeros included, and is used only to cut the Low/Average/High tiers. The report's Average Service Rating uses all 14 attributes and excludes zeros (3.2776), as specified. Both are correct for what they measure; they are different quantities.

## 5. Relationships and filter flow

Star schema, one-to-many, single direction: `DimTravel`, `DimPassengerProfile`, `DimSatisfaction` -> `FactPassenger` and `FactServiceRating`; `DimServiceAttribute` -> `FactServiceRating`; `DimModel` -> model-result tables. Slicers therefore filter passenger and rating visuals and never the SHAP / model-level visuals (those tables have no passenger key). No measure counts rows from the SHAP, tier or model tables.
