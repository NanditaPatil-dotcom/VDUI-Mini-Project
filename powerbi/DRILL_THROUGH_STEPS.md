# Drill-through and Back – steps for the demo

**Open first:** the **EDA & PASSENGER ANALYSIS** tab. Make sure the class chart is at the Class level (Business / Economy / Economy Plus) and drill mode is OFF (see `DRILL_DOWN_STEPS.md`).

1. In the right-hand **Gender** list, click **Female**. (This shows later that your filter is kept.)
2. **Right-click the "Economy" column** of the *Satisfaction by Travel Class* chart.
3. In the menu choose **Drill through → Passenger Segment Detail**.
4. The **Passenger Segment Detail** page opens. Look at its title bar: the large amber **← BACK** button is at the **far left**. The matrix and the rating chart show **Economy + Female** only.
5. **Click ← BACK** (in Power BI Desktop hold **Ctrl** and click; in the Service / Reading view just click).
6. You are back on **EDA & PASSENGER ANALYSIS**, with **Female** still selected.
7. Repeat with **Business** to show the detail page re-filters (and ← BACK again).

Notes
* In Desktop the detail page's tab shows a crossed-eye icon – it is a hidden page (opened only by drill-through). Viewers of the published report do not see that tab.
* The page you return to is the page you came from.
